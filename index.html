Location and Size

The human heart is a vital muscular organ in the circulatory system, pumping blood throughout the body to deliver oxygen and nutrients while removing waste.

~100,000Beats / day
5 LitersBlood / min

Blood Circulation Pathway

In the double circulatory system, deoxygenated blood enters the right side to reach the lungs, then returns oxygenated to the left side to pump body-wide.

Chambers & Valves

Four chambers, valves, and chordae tendineae work together to keep blood flowing in one direction with every heartbeat.
